Weapon settings

Level 0 is preserved where present in your save. Values beyond the game’s normal range are experimental. Rarity controls quality; it does not change the weapon’s combat class or model.

Mods & perks

Use Apply / Remove… beside any perk for a batch across all weapons, melee weapons, or firearms.

The catalog combines named game upgrades with special upgrades found in your save. Each entry includes an effect summary; hover for details. Select All includes the entire catalog, even with filters active, including different damage types and tiers. Untick any you do not want. A stored perk is not a guarantee of an active in-game effect. Duplicate upgrades cannot be added.
