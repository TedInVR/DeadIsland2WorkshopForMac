Weapon settings

Level 0 is preserved where present in your save. Values beyond the game’s normal range are experimental. Rarity controls quality; it does not change the weapon’s combat class or model.

Mods & perks

The catalog combines named game upgrades with special upgrades found in your save. Hover over an entry to see its internal identifier. A stored perk is not a guarantee of an active in-game effect. Duplicate upgrades cannot be added.
